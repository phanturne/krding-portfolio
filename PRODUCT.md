## Positioning

A 50/50 dual-flagship systems showcase where each project leads with authentic high-resolution production UI screenshots and includes a one-click toggle into a live client-side algorithm sandbox:
1. **StressAlpha**: Equity earnings & scenario stress-testing web app covering 521 stocks (`Next.js`, `React`, `TypeScript`, `Tailwind CSS`, `PostgreSQL`, `Drizzle ORM`) — featuring production screenshots (*Dual-Pane Cockpit*, *521-Stock Screener*, *IC Audit Memo*) plus an interactive DCF sensitivity sandbox.
2. **PokéBastion TD**: Open-map roguelike Pokémon tower defense browser game across a 28×18 grid with 152 playable units (`TypeScript`, `Phaser 3`, `Vite`, `HTML5 Canvas`, `Vitest`, `Playwright`) — featuring production screenshots (*Live Wave Combat*, *Maze & Pathfinding*, *Roguelike Draft*) plus an interactive A* wall-routing sandbox.
